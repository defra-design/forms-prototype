Form overview

Notes attached to changes

Research found that internal notes were useful, but people were unsure how to use them to record changes. People looking at form history also wanted to know why a change was made.

What we’re testing

What this concept includes

  • an optional note when making a draft live, replacing a live form, or closing a form
  • an optional note when changing the notification email
  • notes shown on the matching form history entry
  • a separate ‘Add a note’ link for other team information
  • no note needed for everyday edits in the editor

How notes should be written

This follows the useful parts of GOV.UK change note guidance (opens in new tab), adapted for Forms Designer.

Notes should:

  • be specific
  • use full sentences
  • put the most important information first

Unlike GOV.UK change notes, these notes are internal. Form history already records what changed, so the note is mainly for why or other context the team needs.

You do not need a note for small edits, such as fixing typos or minor wording.
