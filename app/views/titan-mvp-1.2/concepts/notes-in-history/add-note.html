Employee Onboarding

Add a note

Use this for information your team needs later, such as handover details or decisions that are not recorded by a change.

Some actions, such as making a form live or closing it, also let you add a note at the same time.

How to write the note

Write a full sentence. Put the most important information first. Be specific about what your team needs to know.

For example:

  • ‘Support phone number is wrong on the live form. Do not make further drafts live until it’s fixed.’
  • ‘Chris is handing this form to Samira from 12 May. Contact Samira for publish decisions.’

Do not write vague notes like ‘Updated’, ‘See Slack’, or ‘As discussed’.
